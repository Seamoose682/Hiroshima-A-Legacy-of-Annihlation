# Hiroshima: A Legacy of Annihilation

Hiroshima is a city with a spacial history deeply rooted in the modern world. In the attached website, we will explore some of the long lasting effects on the world that can be connected to Hiroshima. As well as the significance of Hiroshima itself. Some of the places that we will explore include several of the various buildings within Hiroshima that were preserved or rebuilt following the atomic bombing in 1945, as well as significant historical locations that either played a part in, or where directly affected by the the destruction of Hiroshima.
